  • Weather- and salt-air rated housings and hardware

  • Clean cable runs, sealed connections, labeled ports

Built for island conditions

Cameras that keep working when the weather doesn't.

Salt air, UV, humidity and power fluctuations are hard on electronics. Hardware that's fine on the mainland can corrode or fail within a season here. We choose corrosion-resistant, storm-ready equipment and mount it so water runs away from connections, not into them.

We also plan for the power grid. Ask us about battery backup for your recorder and network gear so footage keeps recording through short outages, and about cloud backup so recordings survive even if on-site equipment is damaged.

Common questions

Security camera questions.

How much does a security camera system cost in St. Thomas?

It depends on the number of cameras, cable distances, mounting, and whether you want cloud backup. We give you a free on-site assessment and a written quote with no hidden fees, so you know the full price before any work starts.

Can I watch my cameras from my phone when I'm off-island?

Yes. We set up the viewing app on your phone during installation so you can see live and recorded footage from anywhere with an internet connection.

Will the cameras hold up to salt air and storms?

We use weather- and salt-air rated hardware, sealed connections, and careful mounting. Ongoing care plans add preventative check-ups to catch corrosion or loose mounts early.

What happens to my recordings if the power goes out?

On-site recording needs power, so we can include battery backup for the recorder and network equipment. Cloud backup keeps a copy of your footage off-site.

Do you install cameras on St. John?

We serve St. Thomas and the wider U.S. Virgin Islands. Call or book an assessment and tell us where your property is.
