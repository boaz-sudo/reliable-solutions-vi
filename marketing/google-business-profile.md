## 6. Review-request messages

Get your review link first: in your Business Profile, click **Ask for reviews** and copy the short link (`https://g.page/r/…/review`). Replace `[REVIEW LINK]` below with it.

**When to ask:** the day the job is finished, while you're still on-site or within a few hours. Ask every customer. Don't offer discounts or gifts for reviews (Google prohibits it), and don't ask only the happy ones.

### Text / WhatsApp (send right after the job)

> Hi [First name], it's [Your name] from Reliable Solutions VI. Thanks for having us out today! If you're happy with your new [cameras / Wi-Fi], would you mind leaving a quick Google review? It really helps other folks on island find us: [REVIEW LINK]
> If anything isn't working right, just reply here and we'll take care of it.

### Email

**Subject:** How did we do, [First name]?

> Hi [First name],
>
> Thank you for choosing Reliable Solutions VI for your [security camera system / network installation]. We hope everything is working well.
>
> We're a small, locally owned business, and most of our new customers find us through Google. If you have two minutes, a short review would mean a lot:
>
> **[REVIEW LINK]**
>
> A sentence or two is plenty. Mentioning what we installed and where (for example, "cameras at our villa in Red Hook") helps neighbors searching for the same thing.
>
> And if anything isn't right, reply to this email or call 340-725-1919 and we'll fix it.
>
> Thank you!
> [Your name]
> Reliable Solutions VI · 340-725-1919

### Gentle follow-up (5–7 days later, only if no review)

> Hi [First name], just checking that your system is still running smoothly. If you have a moment, a quick review would really help us out: [REVIEW LINK]. Thanks again!

### In person

> "Glad it's all working. One favor: we're a local business and Google reviews are how most people find us. I'll text you the link. If you could say a line about the install, that would help a lot."

Also put the review link (or a QR code) on the business card leave-behind and in your email signature.

## 7. Replying to reviews

Reply to every review within a couple of days. Mention the service and location naturally.

**Positive:**
> Thank you, [Name]! It was a pleasure setting up the cameras and Wi-Fi at your place in [area]. We're a call away if you ever need anything. – [Your name], Reliable Solutions VI

**Negative:**
> [Name], I'm sorry this didn't meet your expectations. That's not the experience we want anyone to have. Please call me directly at 340-725-1919 so I can make it right. – [Your name], Reliable Solutions VI

Keep negative replies short and calm, and take the conversation offline.
