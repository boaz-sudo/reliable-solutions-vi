Why St. John is different

Planned around island logistics.

St. John properties are often remote, hillside and exposed to the weather, and a return trip for a missing part costs everyone time. We plan carefully during the assessment, bring what the job needs, and set up remote monitoring and support so many future issues can be handled without a ferry ride.
