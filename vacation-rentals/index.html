Common questions

Vacation rental questions.

Can I check my rental's cameras from the mainland?

Yes. We set up the app on your phone so you can view live and recorded footage anywhere you have internet.

Are cameras allowed at vacation rentals?

Booking platforms set their own rules, which generally allow disclosed exterior cameras and ban cameras in private spaces. We design exterior-only coverage, and you should confirm your platform's current policy.

Can you work with my property manager?

Yes. We can coordinate access and updates with your property manager while keeping you in the loop.

What happens after a storm?

Care-plan clients can request a post-storm check of cameras and network equipment, and our monitoring flags devices that go offline.
