Why it matters

Good Wi-Fi starts with good cable.

Most Wi-Fi problems aren't the internet plan. They're one router trying to push a signal through concrete and across levels. We run cable to well-placed access points so every room, deck and pool area gets a strong signal, and devices hand off smoothly as you move around.

Everything we install is clean, labeled and future-ready, so adding cameras, another building, or a faster internet line later is simple, whoever does the work.

Common questions

Network & Wi-Fi questions.

Why is my Wi-Fi weak in parts of my house?

Concrete walls, multiple levels and distance all block Wi-Fi. A single router rarely covers a whole island home. Wired access points placed in the right spots fix dead zones far better than range extenders.

Do you work with my existing internet provider?

Yes. We install the network inside your property and connect it to the internet service you already have.

What's the difference between mesh Wi-Fi and access points?

Both use several units to cover a property. Connecting each unit by cable gives the most stable, fastest result. We recommend the right mix after seeing your layout.

Can you set up separate networks for guests?

Yes. We set up guest networks that are kept apart from your personal devices, cameras and business systems.

Can my network be managed after installation?

Yes. Our network management plans include monitoring, firmware updates and priority support.
