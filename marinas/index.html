Common questions

Marina questions.

Do cameras survive at the water's edge?

With the right hardware and installation, yes. We use salt-air rated equipment and sealed connections, and recommend regular preventative check-ups for waterfront sites.

Can you cover a long dock or several buildings?

Yes. We design the cabling and wireless links needed to connect cameras across docks, lots and buildings.

Can managers view cameras remotely?

Yes. We set up phone and computer access for the people who need it, with separate logins.

Do you offer Wi-Fi for marina guests?

We can design Wi-Fi coverage for offices, docks and common areas, kept separate from your camera and business networks.
